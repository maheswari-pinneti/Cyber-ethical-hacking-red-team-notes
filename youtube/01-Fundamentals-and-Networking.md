## 🌟 Top Channels & Creators

### 1. NetworkChuck
- **Channel Link:** [youtube.com/@NetworkChuck](https://www.youtube.com/@NetworkChuck)
- **Focus:** Practical networking, CCNA concepts, Wireshark, Linux, Docker, and hands-on IT tutorials.
- **Recommended Playlists:**
  - *Free CCNA 200-301 Course:* Engaging, foundational networking concepts (subnets, routing, switches).
  - *Wireshark Crash Course:* Packet sniffing and network analysis for beginners.

### 2. Professor Messer
- **Channel Link:** [youtube.com/@professormesser](https://www.youtube.com/@professormesser)
- **Focus:** Gold standard for CompTIA Network+ (N10-008) and Security+ (SY0-701) free training courses.
- **Recommended Playlists:**
  - *CompTIA Network+ Training Course:* Complete modular video series covering networking protocols, ports, and topologies.
  - *CompTIA Security+ Training Course:* Comprehensive coverage of security fundamentals.

### 3. David Bombal
- **Channel Link:** [youtube.com/@DavidBombal](https://www.youtube.com/@DavidBombal)
- **Focus:** Networking, Wireshark, Python automation, ethical hacking, and industry interviews.
- **Recommended Playlists:**
  - *Wireshark Ethical Hacking:* In-depth packet analysis, protocol hunting, and traffic analysis.

### 4. freeCodeCamp.org
- **Channel Link:** [youtube.com/@freecodecamp](https://www.youtube.com/@freecodecamp)
- **Focus:** Full 5 to 15 hour university-grade courses on Computer Networking, Operating Systems, and IT basics.
- **Recommended Courses:**
  - *Computer Networking Full Course - OSI Model Deep Dive*
  - *Introduction to Computer Science (CS50 by Harvard)*
